# Best AI tools for Developing/Productivity


## [Tavily](https://docs.tavily.com/documentation/about)

> Tavily is a search engine optimized for LLMs, aimed at efficient, quick and persistent search results. Tailored just for LLM Agents, we ensure the search results are optimized for RAG. We take care of all the burden in searching, scraping, filtering and extracting information from online sources. All in a single API call! Simply pass the returned search results as context to your LLM.


## [Docling](https://docling-project.github.io/docling/)

> Docling simplifies document processing, parsing diverse formats — including advanced PDF understanding — and providing seamless integrations with the gen AI ecosystem.

Docling's ASR (Automatic Speech Recognition) pipeline lets you convert audio and video files into a structured `DoclingDocument`. From there you can export to Markdown, JSON, HTML, or DocTags, and plug the result directly into RAG pipelines, summarizers, or search indexes.


## [OpenRAG](https://www.openr.ag)

> IBM's open-source RAG distribution, powered by OpenSearch, Langflow, and Docling. 

OpenRAG is an open-source package for building agentic RAG systems that integrates with a wide range of orchestration tools, databases, and LLM providers.
